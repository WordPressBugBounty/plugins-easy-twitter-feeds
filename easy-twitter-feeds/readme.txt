### Why Easy Feeds for X/Twitter?

Your social activity shouldn't live only on X. **Easy Feeds for X/Twitter** lets you embed Twitter timelines, posts, videos, hashtags, follow buttons, and tweet buttons on your WordPress site – so visitors stay updated with your latest tweets without leaving your pages. The plugin is lightweight but super powerful.

Customize the timeline's height, width, scrolling behavior, and theme to fit your website's design, and style the Follow Button's color, background, font size, and padding to create a call-to-action that encourages visitors to connect with you on X.

Whether you're a blogger, business owner, or social media enthusiast, Easy Feeds for X/Twitter gives you the tools to strengthen your online presence and connect your website with your audience on X.

**Important Notice: This plugin only works with X premium accounts.**

### Free Features

- **Timeline:** Add a Twitter timeline to any page, post, or widget.

### How to Use

1. Install and activate the **Easy Feeds for X/Twitter** plugin.
2. Add the shortcode to any page, post, or widget – no pre-setup required.
3. To embed a timeline, use `[timeline username="YOUR_USERNAME"]`.
4. To add a follow button, use `[follow_button username="YOUR_USERNAME"]`.
5. Publish – your live feed is on your site!

**Customize the timeline:**

<pre>
[timeline username="YOUR_USERNAME" theme="light" width="300" height="400" title="My Timeline"]
</pre>

* The `theme` attribute accepts `light` and `dark`.
* Don't add "px" to the `height` and `width` values.

**Customize the follow button:**

<pre>
[follow_button username="YOUR_USERNAME" size="large" count="false"]
</pre>

* The `size` attribute accepts `large` and `small`.
* Set `count="true"` to display the total follower count in the button.

To embed feeds in your theme PHP, place `<?php echo do_shortcode( 'YOUR_SHORTCODE' ); ?>` in your template.

== Installation ==

**From your WordPress dashboard (recommended):**

1. Go to **Plugins → Add New**.
2. Search for **"Easy Feeds for X/Twitter"**.
3. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
4. Use the shortcode in any page, post, or widget.

**Manual installation:**

1. Upload the `easy-twitter-feeds` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the **Plugins** menu in WordPress.
3. Use the shortcode in a page, post, or widget.
4. To show feeds in your theme PHP, place `<?php echo do_shortcode( 'YOUR_SHORTCODE' ); ?>` in your template.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need to do any pre-setup? =

No, you don't need to set anything before using the shortcode. Just add it with your username.

= Does this plugin work with any X (Twitter) account? =

No. This plugin only works with X premium accounts.

= How do I embed my Twitter timeline? =

Use the shortcode `[timeline username="YOUR_USERNAME"]` in any page, post, or widget. You can optionally set `theme`, `width`, `height`, and `title` attributes.

= Can I show a follower count on the Follow Button? =

Yes. Add `count="true"` to the follow button shortcode to display the total follower count.

= Can I embed a specific post or video? =

Yes. Embedding specific Twitter posts, videos, and hashtags is available in the Pro version.

= Does it work with the block editor? =

Yes, a Gutenberg block is included, and the shortcode also works in widgets and theme templates.
